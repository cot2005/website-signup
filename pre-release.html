Description of Pre-release service

We are releasing a beta version of our platform for select users to explore the concept while we continue development. During this phase, we are offering free access to our AI-generated trade ideas. Subscribers will receive notifications via email with the trades our algorithms plan for the next trading session. Users retain full control over any investment decisions and must manually place any trades themselves. While we intend to automate portfolio management as we build the platform, this pre-release service is for informational purposes. Beta testers may opt out at any time and are fully responsible for placing trades manually in their own brokerage accounts.
