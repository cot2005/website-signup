🔧 How it Works 🔧

More information on our approach and the Pre-release service we're offering.

Our vision

When I first explored the stock market, I quickly realized investing can be complex, time‑consuming, and risky. Unless you stick with index funds, you risk losing money fast. But if you avoid index funds in hopes of earning more than 6–8% annually, you’re faced with two options: spend hours every day researching and monitoring the market, or take chances on high‑risk assets. Both demand extensive market research—something I didn’t have the time or appetite for.

That’s where the idea for OtisAI began: a platform that sits between passive index investing and high‑risk speculation. It empowers retail investors to potentially grow wealth faster by leveraging AI for investment guidance—even with no background in finance or machine learning. The platform has four main objectives:

  1. Performance: Grow wealth as effectively as possible.
  2. Accessibility: Make the tools affordable and available to everyone.
  3. Simplicity: Everyone can easily use it, regardless of their background.
  4. Transparency: Prove its past success, show everyone why we believe it works, for free.
OtisAI achieves this by scouring the internet for data aligned with a chosen strategy, analyzes it alongside years of historical market data, and uses machine learning models to generate investment signals. These strategies are rigorously backtested, then connected to brokerage APIs for fully automated execution—removing user bias and error.

All an investor needs to do is pick a stock list and select a proven, well‑documented strategy. OtisAI handles the rest: market research, signal generation, and portfolio management. We believe this is just the beginning of a platform that can transform how retail investors build wealth.

Integration of ML models, technical, fundamental, and sentiment data to drive principled strategies that help you grow your investments faster.

What is it right now?

Currently, we have eight modular investment strategies that can be paired with any stock list—of any size—to generate historical performance results for analysis. Each strategy is trained on at least six years of historical data covering over 500 stocks, then validated on two or more years of data across 1,000+ stocks.

We’re continuing to expand our strategy library by incorporating new types of market data and exploring novel analytical approaches that can inform better financial decisions. These innovations will drive the creation of even more strategies over time.

For automated portfolio management, we have begun experimenting with Alpaca Securities as our initial brokerage (with more to come). With an Alpaca account, our platform can execute trades and manage portfolios in line with the selected strategy. This can also be done manually, as most strategies average fewer than ten orders per week.

Using this approach, we’ve been able to prototype and test our strategies—and combinations of them—in Alpaca accounts, consistently achieving returns that outperform the market.

Description of Pre-release service

Since we want to give people early access to try the concept while we build the platform, we can initially offer a limited-time email service for $5.00 per portfolio per month. Subscribers would receive notifications with the trade orders our algorithms plan to execute for the next trading session. Users could then manually place these trades through their own brokerage accounts (e.g., Robinhood, Schwab, TD Ameritrade). While we cannot provide automated asset management at this stage, our intention is to include this feature in the full platform.
