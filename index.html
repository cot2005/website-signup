How to use it

1. Pick stocks you like

Choose from preset lists of stocks we offer or construct a custom list of stocks you would like to invest in, trade, or track for possible future investment. This list creates the foundation of options that your selected algorithm tracks and is permitted to invest in.

2. Choose an AI driven strategy

Choose an investment strategy from our catalog of numerous validated strategies that fit your risk tolerance, preferred investment timeframe, and desired returns. This sets the parameters for what the algorithm identifies as opportunities.

3. Avest-AI predicts Opportunities

The platform monitors market data for the stocks in your list at a frequency defined by your chosen strategy, then uses our trained models to predict buy and sell opportunities for your portfolio.

4. Avest-AI manages your portfolio automatically

When opportunities are identified, you are notified of upcoming changes to your portfolio. Orders are then automatically executed in your linked brokerage account during the next trading session.

What is it right now?

Currently, we have eight modular investment strategies that can be paired with any stock list—of any size—to generate historical performance results for evaluation. Each strategy is trained on at least six years of historical data covering over 500 stocks, then validated on two or more years of data across 1,000+ stocks.

We’re continuing to expand our strategy catalog by incorporating new types of market data and exploring novel approaches that can inform greater investment decisions. These innovations will drive the creation of even more strategies over time.

For automated portfolio management, we have begun experimenting with Alpaca Securities as an initial brokerage (with more to come). With an Alpaca account, our platform can execute trades and manage portfolios in line with the selected strategy. This can also be done manually, as most strategies average fewer than ten stock orders per week.

Using this approach, we’ve been able to prototype and test our strategies, and combinations of them, in Alpaca accounts, consistently achieving returns that outperform the market.
